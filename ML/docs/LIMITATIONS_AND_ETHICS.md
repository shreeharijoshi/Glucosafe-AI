# Limitations and Ethics

The candidate datasets contain few participants, imbalanced outcomes, missing or noisy data, dataset-specific sampling, and uncertain event semantics. High offline performance may reflect participant-specific patterns, repeated overlapping windows, or recording artifacts rather than generalizable risk prediction.

False negatives and false positives can both be harmful. This prototype must not diagnose hypoglycemia, recommend insulin or food doses, guarantee safety, or replace professional care. Independent validation, safety review, privacy controls, and clinical oversight are required before any real-world use.

Health-related data must be stored securely, access must be limited, and generated artifacts must not expose unnecessary participant information.
